# SCRIPT — interview-copilot-demo

**Voice:** af_heart (Kokoro, offline) — curated "product demo" pick
**Voice settings:** default speed
**Voice direction:** Calm, warm, plain-spoken. A friend showing you a tool, not an ad. Small pauses at the dashes.

---

## Line 1 — Mind goes blank (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 4.5s
**Delivery:** Quiet, empathetic; let "blank" hang.

    They ask a tough question. Your mind goes blank.

## Line 2 — Meet Interview Copilot (Frame 2)

**Time:** 4.5 – 11.0s
**Delivery:** Brighter; the promise lands on "on screen".

    Meet Interview Copilot. About two seconds after they finish, your answer is on screen.

## Line 3 — Your files (Frame 3)

**Time:** 11.0 – 17.0s
**Delivery:** Practical, step one.

    First, add your CV and the job post. Every answer is built from them.

## Line 4 — Two channels (Frame 4)

**Time:** 17.0 – 25.5s
**Delivery:** Clear, one beat per sentence.

    Press Record. Your mic is Me. Your computer's sound is the Interviewer. Two channels, so it always knows who's talking.

## Line 5 — Live transcript (Frame 5)

**Time:** 25.5 – 30.0s
**Delivery:** Light.

    Their words show up live, as they speak.

## Line 6 — It spots the question (Frame 6)

**Time:** 30.0 – 35.0s
**Delivery:** Two halves, contrast.

    Small talk gets skipped. A real question gets flagged.

## Line 7 — The answer streams in (Frame 7)

**Time:** 35.0 – 43.0s
**Delivery:** Satisfied; slow down on "your own CV".

    And your answer streams in. The key line highlighted, talking points from your own CV underneath.

## Line 8 — Read it from your phone (Frame 8)

**Time:** 35.7 – 41.7s
**Delivery:** Easy, reassuring.

    On your phone, it's a dim teleprompter. It follows your voice and keeps your place as you read.

## Line 9 — About two seconds (Frame 9)

**Time:** 41.7 – 45.6s
**Delivery:** Confident, measured.

    Their last word to your first line: about two seconds.

## Line 10 — Press Record (Frame 10)

**Time:** 45.6 – 50.8s
**Delivery:** Warm invitation.

    Interview Copilot. Try the demo interview, then press Record.
